# ARE Intelligence

Regulatory decisions connect documents to operators, obligations and supporting evidence. ARE Intelligence organizes those relationships for Mauritania’s regulator through a shared evidence base, source inspection and decision-support views in French and Arabic.

A regulatory claim needs a traceable source and version. The same evidence must also remain usable across appropriate access boundaries and an Arabic right-to-left interface, without creating separate information models for each language.

The platform foundation combines three mechanisms:

- Source intake separates original files and candidate text from approved evidence. Quarantine and review keep an imported document distinguishable from an authoritative regulatory source.
- Obligations and assessments link back to supporting material. Reviewers can move between a requirement, the evidence used to assess it and the corresponding decision workspace.
- A modular backend shares its business logic with background workers. Document processing can progress separately from interactive work, while the interface provides source-oriented navigation and regulatory views.

ARE currently uses lexical retrieval; generated assistant answers are disabled, and platform acceptance remains ongoing.

The work is developed collaboratively within Richat Partners’ AI and software team, with product and domain contributors shaping the regulatory workflows.

**Technology:** FastAPI, SQLAlchemy, PostgreSQL/pgvector, Procrastinate, React, Mantine and ECharts.
