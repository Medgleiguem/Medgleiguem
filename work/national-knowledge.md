# National Knowledge Base

The National Knowledge Base prepares Mauritanian documents and datasets for evidence-based applications. Its staff workspace connects source imports, private document inspection and initial dataset review, with reviewed, citable retrieval as the longer-term product direction.

An extracted preview can be useful to inspect without being ready to publish. Structured evidence also needs its units, periods and source context preserved: accurately copied characters do not guarantee a correctly interpreted number.

Three mechanisms keep those distinctions visible:

- Imports retain provenance and verified original bytes. Private library entries and previews support staff inspection without acquiring approval status merely because they are accessible.
- Extraction produces inspectable text and structured artifacts. Partial results and processing holds remain explicit, preserving the distinction between an original, a derived preview and a reviewed interpretation.
- Dataset review records whether checking covered all values or a declared sample, alongside findings and limitations. Recording an approval and activating a customer release are separate operations.

The current workspace supports unreviewed library previews and initial dataset-draft decisions; document approval, publication and customer retrieval are separate increments.

A collaborative Richat Partners effort involving software engineering and evidence-review workflows.

**Technology:** Django, Django REST Framework, PostgreSQL/pgvector, React and a separate local Docling PDF worker.
