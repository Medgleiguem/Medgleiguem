## Selected work

### Institutional AI & knowledge systems

At **Richat Partners**, I lead AI and software delivery for the Ministry of Health, the Central Bank of Mauritania and the telecom regulator, ARE. The work includes RAG assistants, decision dashboards and a national knowledge base of **100,000+ documents**.

The engineering focus: verified sources, amendment-aware legal retrieval, linked French/Arabic/English material, document versioning and expert review before publication.

<sub>RAG · LLM agents · Legal NLP · Document pipelines · Retrieval APIs</sub>

### Payments, identity & national data

At **IBTIKAR**, I built systems for Taazour Aoun and Taazour Pay: donation programs reaching **2M+ citizens**, with **5.5B MRO** distributed and bank integration through GIMTEL. My identity work combined liveness detection, facial matching and Houwiyeti OTP verification for banking workflows.

For **ANRPTS**, I built an analytics platform connecting five data sources through Airflow, dbt and PostgreSQL to a 22-module Next.js dashboard. This final-year project earned **19.75/20**.

<sub>Fintech · Computer vision · Airflow · dbt · PostgreSQL · Next.js</sub>

### AI Diwan — Arabic poetry, with an AI companion

A structured collection of poems, poets and verses, with **Al-Hakim**, an AI assistant for exploring Arabic poetry. A personal project connecting language, culture and product engineering.

[Visit AI Diwan](https://el-mejlise.vercel.app)

### Sonora — your music, on your device

An open-source Android app and web player with a shared React interface, a local offline library and native Android background playback. Actively developed; local music is the core experience, while online streaming and downloading remain experimental.

[Source & documentation](https://github.com/Medgleiguem/sonora-music-hub) · [Web player](https://sonora-music-hub-six.vercel.app) · [Android downloads](https://sonora-music-hub-six.vercel.app/android)

<sub>React · Next.js · Capacitor · Android Media3 · GPL-3.0</sub>
